About

Most of what I make starts with a device I own and a piece of software I wish existed for it. My Mac had no good system-wide equalizer, so I wrote one in Rust on top of Core Audio process taps, with no audio driver to install. My consoles sat by the TV while I wanted to play from another room, so I wrote a capture-card streamer that forwards MJPEG frames untouched and decodes them once, on the Mac, with about 12 ms of delay.

I like the parts of software that sit near hardware: audio pipelines, USB protocols, V4L2 and UVC capture, and the native UI layers on macOS and Android. I also build web apps when a problem calls for one, from a booking site for a wellness practice to an offline billing system for a pool hall.

In the age of AI, I believe what matters is what you complete and ship, and how well you ship it. Anyone can start something now. Finishing it, polishing it and putting it in someone's hands is the part that counts.

So each project ships with releases, screenshots in both themes and documentation that explains how the thing works, not just how to install it.

Featured projects

EQ for macOS

A free system-wide parametric equalizer for every sound your Mac plays. No audio driver, no kernel extension, no account. Drag points on a frequency-response curve and hear the change at once.

  • Rust
  • egui
  • Core Audio process taps
  • DSP
  • Apple silicon + Intel

capture-stream

Play a PS4, PS5, Switch or Xbox from another room. A USB capture card on a Linux box forwards MJPEG frames unchanged to a native Mac app that decodes them with Apple's hardware JPEG decoder. About 12 ms of video delay at 1080p60.

  • Swift
  • SwiftUI
  • V4L2 / UVC
  • MJPEG
  • Linux daemon
Repository Website Download v0.2.0

EQ for Android

A system-wide equalizer for other apps' audio on a stock phone, no root. Graphic mode with up to 31 bands or parametric mode with up to 16, plus a preamp, a limiter, undo and a bypass switch in the notification.

  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose
  • Rust core
  • DynamicsProcessing
  • Android 9+

JA11 EQ

A small macOS app that programs the on-board five-band parametric EQ of the FiiO JadeAudio JA11 USB DAC dongle over USB. The curve lives in the dongle, so it follows you to a phone or another computer. It replaces FiiO's own configuration app on the Mac.

  • Swift
  • USB HID
  • Swift Package
  • macOS 14+
